# SDG 11, 12 & 13: Sustainable Cities, Responsible Consumption, Climate Action

*Authored for this project — Sustainability Intelligence DSS*

## SDG 11: Sustainable Cities and Communities

SDG 11 calls for making cities inclusive, safe, resilient, and sustainable. Electricity consumption is a core component of urban sustainability. London, as one of the world's largest cities, has set a target of net zero carbon by 2030.

**Relevance to this project**: The ACORN classification used in this dataset maps household consumption patterns to community types (Affluent, Comfortable, Adversity). Comparing consumption across ACORN groups reveals spatial patterns of energy use and identifies communities where efficiency programmes could be most impactful. The "Adversity" group often faces energy poverty — high consumption relative to income — making targeted support programmes doubly important.

## SDG 12: Responsible Consumption and Production

SDG 12 targets significantly reducing waste and encouraging sustainable consumption patterns. For electricity:

- **Target 12.8**: Ensure all people have relevant information and awareness for sustainable development and lifestyles. This dashboard directly supports this target by making consumption data legible and actionable.
- **Target 12.c**: Rationalise inefficient energy subsidies. Understanding who benefits most from flat-rate vs Time-of-Use tariffs informs policy design.

## SDG 13: Climate Action

SDG 13 calls for urgent action to combat climate change. Household electricity decarbonisation is a direct lever:

- Each kWh shifted from gas-peaking periods to renewable-rich periods reduces net CO₂ emissions.
- The CO₂ figures shown throughout this dashboard quantify the climate impact of current consumption patterns and the potential savings from recommended actions.
- The what-if impact calculator enables households and policymakers to model the collective CO₂ reduction from behaviour change at scale.
