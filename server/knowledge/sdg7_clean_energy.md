# SDG 7: Affordable and Clean Energy

*Authored for this project — Sustainability Intelligence DSS*

## Goal Description

UN Sustainable Development Goal 7 (SDG 7) calls for ensuring access to affordable, reliable, sustainable, and modern energy for all by 2030. For developed nations like the UK, it focuses on the clean and sustainable dimensions: decarbonising existing electricity systems, improving energy efficiency, and reducing the energy burden on low-income households.

## Relevance to Household Electricity Analysis

### Target 7.3 — Double the Rate of Energy Efficiency Improvement

Household electricity consumption patterns reveal efficiency opportunities. Identifying clusters of households with high peak-hour usage or high standby loads enables targeted outreach that contributes to the efficiency improvement rate.

### Target 7.2 — Increase the Share of Renewables

By shifting demand to periods when renewable generation (especially wind at night) is higher, households can effectively increase their renewable consumption share without any change to the grid infrastructure.

## KPIs in This Dashboard

- **Average kWh per household per day**: tracks the efficiency of the sampled population.
- **CO₂ per household per day**: directly measures the SDG 7 decarbonisation contribution.
- **Peak share**: households with high peak-share are disproportionate contributors to the need for gas peaking plant and represent the highest-impact efficiency opportunity.
